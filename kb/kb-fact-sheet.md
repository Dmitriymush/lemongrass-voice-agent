# Lemongrass Kitchen — Fact Sheet

Modern Thai restaurant. Casual dining. Reservations recommended on Friday and Saturday evenings.

## Contact and location

- Address: 214 Harbor Street, Portland, Maine 04101
- Phone: (207) 555-0148
- Parking: free two-hour street parking on Harbor Street after 6 pm. Public garage at 45 Fore Street, one block away, five dollars flat rate in the evening.

## Opening hours

- Monday: closed
- Tuesday to Thursday: 5 pm to 10 pm
- Friday and Saturday: 5 pm to 11 pm
- Sunday: 4 pm to 9 pm
- Kitchen closes 30 minutes before closing time.

## Menu highlights and prices

- Pad thai with shrimp or tofu: 19 dollars. Contains peanuts.
- Green curry with chicken or vegetables: 21 dollars
- Massaman beef curry: 24 dollars. Contains peanuts.
- Whole crispy sea bass with tamarind sauce: 34 dollars
- Papaya salad: 13 dollars. Contains peanuts and fish sauce.
- Mango sticky rice: 9 dollars

Full menu available at the restaurant. Prices exclude tax and gratuity.

## Dietary information

- Vegetarian and vegan options are available for most dishes on request.
- Gluten-free options are available; gluten-free soy sauce is used on request.
- The kitchen uses peanuts, tree nuts, shellfish, soy and fish sauce. Dishes are prepared in a shared kitchen. Guests with allergies should inform their server before ordering.

## Reservations and groups

- Reservations are taken by phone. Tables are held for 15 minutes past the reservation time.
- Parties up to 8 can be requested by phone.
- Parties of more than 8, and private events, are handled by the events manager. Leave your name and phone number and the events manager will call back. Private dining room seats up to 20.
- Cancellation: please cancel at least 24 hours ahead. No cancellation fee.
- Walk-ins are welcome when tables are available.

## Other

- Dress code: casual.
- Children are welcome. High chairs available. No dedicated kids menu; half portions of pad thai and green curry available on request.
- Payment: all major credit cards, Apple Pay, cash. No checks.
- Wheelchair accessible entrance and restroom.
